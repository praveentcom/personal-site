DNSDeck is a native DNS management client for macOS, iOS, iPadOS, and visionOS that lets users manage DNS records across Cloudflare, Amazon Route 53, Vercel, and Google Cloud DNS from a single app. It supports all major record types including A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, SRV, PTR, CAA, and NS, with built-in validation and zone import/export for backups and provider migrations.

Built with SwiftUI, the app stores API credentials securely in iOS Keychain with iCloud sync. It is available as a one-time purchase on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dnsdeck-manage-dns-zones/id6753925998) with lifetime updates.
